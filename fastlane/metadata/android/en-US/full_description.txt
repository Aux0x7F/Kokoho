<p>Kokoho is a free, open-source, offline-first community situational awareness app for Android that passively monitors public agency radio frequencies, P25 trunking control channels, fleet Wi-Fi/Bluetooth hardware identifiers (OUIs), and ADS-B aircraft broadcasts.</p>

<p><b>At-a-Glance Monitor:</b> Full-screen status color and concise agency identification for DHS, CBP, ICE, HSI, AMO, and FPS by default, with optional local Police, Sheriff, Fire, and Search &amp; Rescue toggles.</p>

<p><b>Offline-First &amp; Tracker-Free:</b> Estimates signal distance and multi-point triangulation directly on your phone with zero Google Play Services, no accounts, and no tracking. Optional community observation sharing processes signals ephemerally in volatile memory without storing user locations.</p>

<p>Source code: https://github.com/Aux0x7F/Kokoho</p>
